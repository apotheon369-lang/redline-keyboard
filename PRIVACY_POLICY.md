# Privacy Policy — Redline Keyboard

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2026  
**Developer:** apotheon369-lang  
**Package:** `dev.redline.keyboard`

Redline Keyboard is built to be **100% free, ad-free, and privacy-respecting**.

## What Data We Collect
**None.** Redline Keyboard does **not** operate any analytics servers, ad SDKs, crash trackers, or telemetry pipelines.

- **Keystrokes & Learned Words:** All typing suggestions, autocorrect models, swipe templates, and learned word frequencies stay strictly on your device in local memory/storage. Password fields are explicitly excluded from learning, suggestions, and key popups.
- **Clipboard History:** Stored locally on your device only. Android 13+ sensitive clipboard entries (such as passwords and OTP codes) are automatically skipped.
- **Microphone / Voice Dictation (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Only accessed when you explicitly tap the **`🎙`** microphone button to dictate text. Audio is routed directly to your Android device's configured system speech recognition service (on-device or system provider) — Redline Keyboard never records, stores, or transmits your audio to any Redline server.
- **Custom Fonts:** When you import a `.ttf`/`.otf` font via Android's document picker, only that single selected file is copied into Redline's private app storage.

## Ads & Monetization
Redline Keyboard contains **zero ads**, zero in-app purchases, and zero tracking SDKs.
